Reading The ruler marks one unit at a time, in PDFs, EPUBs and saved web pages. Move it with [ and ], or click a line to jump to the unit under the pointer. All of this is also on the ruler button in the reader toolbar — right-click it. Step by Word Line Sentence Paragraph Highlight Style Tint Rounded Soft Marker Underline Dim rest Colour Strength Breathing room How much room the highlight leaves around the text, as a share of the built-in amount. Displayed equations get a good deal more of it sideways than running prose does. Tint is a flat wash; Rounded takes the corners off; Soft feathers the edges so they fade out; Marker is a chisel-tip stroke with slanted ends; Underline puts nothing over the text at all; and Dim rest veils the rest of the page instead. In EPUBs and web pages the shaped styles come out as the flat tint. Keep the ink on top Blends the highlight with the page so the glyphs keep their full contrast, the way a marker pen leaves the ink alone. Turn it off to lay a flat wash over the text instead. Scrolling Scroll the page Never Only when the unit is off screen On every step Keep clear of top How far down the window the unit lands when the page does scroll, as a share of the window height — so it never sits flush against the top edge with nothing to lead into. Sentences Fold display equations into the surrounding sentence Off by default: a displayed formula is its own stop, so …such that, the equation, and where… are three separate steps. Turn this on to read them as one sentence instead. Clicking the page Moves the ruler Moves the ruler with Cmd/Ctrl held Does nothing A plain click is also how text is selected, so hold the modifier instead if the ruler keeps landing somewhere you did not mean. Annotating Shortcut Option/Alt + H Cmd/Ctrl + Shift + H Cmd/Ctrl + Shift + U Off Marks what the ruler is on — or whatever you have selected — as a Zotero highlight or underline. The shortcut opens a small panel: the digits 1–8 pick a colour, h and u the kind of mark, Enter makes it, and n makes it and opens Zotero's own popup for a comment and tags. Cmd/Ctrl + C copies the sentence the ruler is on Only when nothing is selected — with a selection the copy key is the reader's own, and copies that.
